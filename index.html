How we work

Five steps. After each one you review the result and approve it.

Kickoff 1 day

We discuss the goal, audience, timeline and budget. You get a quote and a stage-by-stage plan.

Wireframes 2-4 days

The skeleton of pages or screens: what goes where and how people reach the goal.

Design 3-7 days

The final look in your style, for phone and desktop. Revisions until you approve.

Development 5-14 days

Front end and back end, forms, payments, analytics. You see a working version along the way.

Launch 1 day

Publishing, testing on devices, handing over access and a short guide.

FAQ

How long does a project take?

A landing page usually takes one to three weeks. For a store or an app I give a timeline after the brief and fix dates for every stage.

Can I order only design or only development?

Yes. I can design mockups for your team or build a design you already have.

Why are prices public?

So you know the ballpark right away. The final price depends on scope and is fixed in the quote before we start.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of the task, a few examples you like, and any materials you have: logo, copy, photos.

Do you support the project after launch?

Yes. I fix bugs and add pages and features. We agree on the support format separately.

Tell me about your project

I reply within a day, ask a couple of questions and send a free estimate.
